What the scan options mean

Host discovery

Checks whether hosts appear reachable without performing a port scan.

SYN scan (-sS)

Examines TCP ports using SYN probes; often called a half-open scan.

Version detection (-sV)

Probes open ports to identify the services and versions listening there.

OS detection (-O)

Uses TCP/IP fingerprinting to estimate the target operating system.

Ports

Open ports indicate services accepting connections; they are not proof of a vulnerability.

Defensive use

Inventory approved assets, reduce unnecessary exposure, and verify firewall rules.

Responsible use

Only scan systems you own or have explicit permission to assess. Unauthorized scanning may violate policy or law. The addresses in this demo are reserved for documentation and the simulator does not perform real network activity.

For the real Nmap reference guide and installation instructions, visit the official Nmap documentation.
